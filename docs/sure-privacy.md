# Privacy Policy — Sure Self-Hosted

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This application is a private, self-hosted instance of the open-source personal
finance app [Sure](https://github.com/we-promise/sure). It is operated by one
individual, for that individual's own use only. It is not a commercial service
and has no other users.

## What data is processed

Account balances and transaction history retrieved, with explicit consent, from
the account holder's own bank accounts via Enable Banking's PSD2 APIs.

## Where it is stored

On a private server controlled solely by the operator. Sensitive fields are
encrypted at rest. The instance is not reachable from the public internet.

## Who it is shared with

No one. Data is never sold, shared, transmitted to third parties, or used for
advertising, profiling, or analytics.

## Retention and deletion

Data is retained until the operator deletes it. Bank consent can be revoked at
any time through the bank or by deleting the connection in the application,
which ends all further data retrieval.
